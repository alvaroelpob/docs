## Escrow Rules [#escrow-rules]

These rules apply to every transaction handled through AutoPlus escrow.

<ol className="list-decimal list-inside ml-4 space-y-2">
  <li>Users are responsible for carefully reviewing all bot prompts and confirmations before proceeding, including deal details, payment amounts, networks, addresses, usernames, and any other transaction information. Users are also responsible for securing access to their Discord account and AutoPlus password if one is set. AutoPlus is not liable for losses caused by user error, incorrect addresses, wrong networks, compromised accounts, mistaken confirmations, or failure to follow instructions. These mistakes may result in permanent loss of funds.</li>
  <li>AutoPlus advocates for neutrality, which means we do not generally forbid specific types of trades between users. However, AutoPlus does not endorse trades that are unethical, suspicious, fraudulent, unsafe, or against Discord's Terms of Service. If a trade appears to violate these rules or creates unreasonable risk, staff may withdraw from handling the dispute, and the users involved may be required to resolve the matter between themselves.</li>
  <li>Both parties must remain active and responsive during a transaction or dispute. If one party becomes unresponsive, AutoPlus may reserve the right to release the funds to the appropriate party based on the available information. Funds will not be released before at least 3 hours after a dispute is opened, and the usual response timeframe is up to 24 hours depending on the situation.</li>
  <li>Sellers are allowed to set their own terms of service, but these terms must be clear, reasonable, and fair to both parties. If a buyer fails to follow the seller's terms, this does <strong>not</strong> automatically give the seller the right to keep the funds. However, if the buyer's actions create a clear loss or risk for the seller, and the terms were clearly communicated before the deal, the seller may be entitled to keep the funds. Any abusive, hidden, misleading, or unfair terms designed to exploit buyers may be overruled by staff.</li>
</ol>

## Creating a trade [#creating-a-trade]

There are two ways to start an AutoPlus trade. The first way is to go to the <DiscordChannelMention>escrow</DiscordChannelMention> channel and use the message dropdown to select the cryptocurrency of the deal. After the crypto is selected, AutoPlus creates the trade flow inside a private thread. The second way is to use the `/trade <user> <cryptocurrency>` slash command, where `<user>` is the other party in the deal and `<cryptocurrency>` is the cryptocurrency used for the trade.

## Identifying the sender and receiver [#identifying-the-sender-and-receiver]

Once the thread is created each user has to select either `I am the Sender` or `I am the Receiver` in the thread.

<ul className="list-disc list-inside">
  <li>The <strong>Sender</strong> is the user who sends crypto to the escrow.</li>
  <li>The <strong>Receiver</strong> is the user who receives the funds after deal completion.</li>
</ul>

<Block color="red">
  <p>Be careful: only the <strong>Sender</strong> can release funds, and only the <strong>Receiver</strong> can return funds. Make sure both roles match the actual deal before moving forward.</p>
</Block>

## Setting trade terms [#setting-trade-terms]
Before AutoPlus provides the deposit address, both users can write the terms of the deal. They appear as <strong>Sender's Terms</strong> and <strong>Receiver's Terms</strong>, and each can be up to 896 characters. Both users must accept the terms before the trade can continue. Once accepted, the terms cannot be modified.


<Block color="blue">
  <p>Trading without terms is possible but discouraged: if there is a dispute, staff has nothing to base a decision on. Terms must also be fair to both sides (see <a href="#escrow-rules" className="text-primary hover:underline">Escrow Rule 4</a>).</p>
</Block>

## Sending funds into the escrow [#sending-funds-into-the-escrow]

After terms are accepted, AutoPlus provides a deposit address. The <strong>Sender</strong> sends the agreed amount of the selected cryptocurrency to the provided address.

<Block color="red">
  <p>Before sending, double-check:</p>
  <ul className="list-disc list-inside ml-4 space-y-1 mt-2">
    <li>You are sending to the address shown <strong>inside your trade thread</strong>. You can confirm it belongs to AutoPlus on the <a href="/check-address" className="text-primary hover:underline">Check Address</a> page.</li>
    <li>You are sending on the <strong>same network</strong> selected for the trade. Funds sent to the wrong address or network may be lost <u><strong>permanently</strong></u>.</li>
  </ul>
</Block>

<img src="/trade-system/sending-funds.png" alt="Sending funds into the escrow." width="762" height="491" className="mt-2 max-w-full md:max-w-[80%] 2xl:max-w-[800px]" />

Once AutoPlus detects the transaction, it shows it as <strong>Confirming</strong> until the blockchain reaches the <a href="#supported-cryptocurrencies" className="text-primary hover:underline">required number of confirmations</a> for that cryptocurrency.

<Block color="red">
  <p><strong>The Receiver should not deliver anything while the transaction is still confirming.</strong> A trade is only funded once AutoPlus marks the funds as received.</p>
</Block>

## Transaction not detected [#transaction-not-detected]

If the <strong>Sender</strong> has sent the funds but AutoPlus hasn't detected them, the <strong>Sender</strong> can click the `Transaction not detected` button. AutoPlus will ask for the transaction ID and look it up. If the transaction still isn't found, <a href="#asking-for-support" className="text-primary hover:underline">ask for support</a> in the thread and share the transaction ID.

## Funds received [#funds-received]

Once the required confirmations are reached, the funds are marked as received. Funds are now held in the escrow by AutoPlus. The <strong>Receiver</strong> can now deliver the product, service, exchange, or whatever was agreed.

<Block color="red">
  <p>The Receiver should only trust this message when AutoPlus posts it <strong>inside your trade thread</strong>. Never deliver based on a screenshot, a DM, or the other user saying they have paid.</p>
</Block>

## Releasing funds [#releasing-funds]

Only the **Sender** can release funds. When they do, AutoPlus asks the <strong>Receiver</strong> for their address and sends the funds to it.

<Block color="red">
  <p><strong>Releasing funds is final.</strong> Only release once you have fully verified that the deal was completed as agreed.</p>
</Block>

## Returning funds [#returning-funds]

Only the **Receiver** can return funds. When they do, AutoPlus asks the <strong>Sender</strong> for their address and sends the funds to it.

## Reducing the trade amount [#reducing-the-trade-amount]

Reduce amount can be started by both parties. It is used when both users agree that the **Receiver** should receive less than the original escrowed amount. The user selects a new reduced amount, and both parties must agree to the new amount before it is applied. The difference is returned to the **Sender**, and the trade continues with the reduced amount.

## Asking for support [#asking-for-support]

Either party can ask for support from the trade thread. Staff are pinged and join the thread, where they review the trade, ask for clarification, and help resolve the issue. After clarification, funds may be force released or force returned depending on the outcome.

To help support resolve it quickly:

<ul className="list-disc list-inside">
  <li>Explain the problem clearly in the thread.</li>
  <li>Share any proof you have, such as screenshots, recordings, or transaction IDs.</li>
  <li>Stay active and respond to staff. If one party stops responding, staff may decide based on the information available.</li>
</ul>

## Trade completed [#trade-completed]

After funds are released or returned, the trade is complete and:

<ul className="list-disc list-inside">
  <li>Both parties get a <strong>transcript</strong> of the trade in their DMs.</li>
  <li>Both parties earn <a href="/docs/points-system" className="text-primary hover:underline">points</a> based on the escrow fee paid.</li>
  <li>The deal appears in the <DiscordChannelMention>transactions</DiscordChannelMention> channel. To hide your username there, use the `/settings` command and click the `Toggle anonymity` button.</li>
</ul>
